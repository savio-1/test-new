# A Friend of Mine (Melbourne)

URL: https://afom.com.au. An integrated brand and digital studio founded by Suzy Tuxen.

## Philosophy
"Weaving craft with strategy, we create utterly unique brands and experiences underpinned by a powerful digital presence." "We don't have a house style." The studio is thoughtful, friendly, rigorous and bespoke. It works across e-commerce brands, hospitality and not-for-profits. The tone is warm, witty and optimistic: brands "soft around the edges".

## Typography
- The site is set in **Banana Grotesk** at 24/30 and 28/35, regular weight, normal tracking, sentence case. Project titles use an em dash: "Great Wrap —".
- Client work leans on **ultra-condensed grotesque caps**:
  - GREAT WRAP, set very tight and black;
  - W.H.M in yellow;
  - HOMMEY. in a fat, rounded display.
- These are paired with a neat neo-grotesk for small information. The headline usually fills 80–100% of the container width.

## Layout & grid
- The homepage opens on a full-bleed 100vh image hero with the title and tagline at bottom left.
- Below that, a 2-column image grid (50/50, no gutter) and centred statement paragraphs of about 40% width in generous white space.
- Case pages:
  - hero 100% × 90vh;
  - title at top left (about 2%);
  - intro text block at about 18–55%;
  - a "Scope:" list at about 57–70%, small.
- Then come full-bleed pairs and 4 × 2 tile mosaics with no gutters, plus giant headlines between them ("Greater than good.").
- Posters are shown flat on white: the type block takes the bottom 25%, the photo the top 65%, with small meta text at the top corners.

## Colour
Saturated, cheerful primaries and pastels:
- cobalt (#1E3FD8), sky (#38B6F0), fuchsia, yellow (#FFE01B);
- burnt orange and sage for Great Wrap;
- retro tomato red for Hommey.
Large flat colour backdrops appear in the photography itself.

## Imagery
- Studio colour-block photography: models in single-colour outfits against contrasting seamless backdrops.
- Surreal, playful product sculptures, like stacked rolls with googly eyes.
- Macro food under cling wrap.
- Real interiors and signage photographed in situ.

## Signature moves
- Colour-blocked studio photography: subject colour against a contrasting backdrop.
- An ultra-condensed caps wordmark spanning the full width.
- Gutterless 4-column mosaics mixing type tiles and photos.
- Playful props and characters that humanise the product.
- Die-cut or tactile packaging details (Lune's "hyperdrive" vent slots).
- Social tiles: a big condensed word plus a small photo, in rotating colours.
- An em-dash title format: "Client — one-line idea".

## 3 notable projects
- **Lune Croissanterie (2015–16):** a redrawn rocket mark with a croissant tail, kraft boxes with die-cut "hyperdrive" ventilation and glitter print, and a cut-steel blade sign.
- **Great Wrap (c.2022):** repositioned as a materials-science company for the US launch. GREAT WRAP condensed caps, character sculptures, posters and e-commerce.
- **Women's Health Melbourne (c.2024):** the apostrophe replaced by an egg motif, condensed type, fuchsia/cobalt/yellow portraiture and the "Knocked Up" podcast.
