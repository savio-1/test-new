# All Purpose (London)

URL: https://allpurpose.studio. The studio was founded as Our Place in 2014 by Ted Heffernan and Alex Gross and renamed All Purpose in 2021. It also runs a type foundry (AllPurpose Fonts) and a publishing imprint (Lichen Books).

## Philosophy
The studio calls itself "a design studio focused on branding and digital experiences" whose work is "direct and uncompromising". It is culture-first: fashion, music, wine, magazines, art institutions. It has no fixed house aesthetic. Each project gets an attitude, usually a loud one, and is built on custom type. The name and its humour come from everyday materials.

## Typography
- The site is set entirely in the in-house **AP All Purpose Grotesk**: 14px/700 for navigation and captions, 32px/900 for intros, 112px/900 uppercase for display. Tracking is a tight −0.14px.
- Client work runs on custom or heavily art-directed display faces:
  - a lurid stacked serif for Ancestrel;
  - a heavy-metal, glitch-split blackletter for the RA Schools Show;
  - squeezed condensed caps in cyan or magenta for Noble Rot spreads, set against old-style serifs and blackletter.
- Display type is set huge and often condensed. It is stacked 2–3 lines deep and usually bleeds or overprints the image.

## Layout & grid
- The homepage is a scattered, asymmetric 2-column field. Images are about 33% wide and sit alternately left and right, with captions in 9px bold under each one, in very generous white space.
- Case pages open with a full-bleed hero (about 100% wide × 56% of the viewport). Then comes an intro in 32px black weight, left-aligned and about 45% wide, then a horizontal carousel with a progress dash bar, then mosaic grids of 4 × 2 tight square crops.
- The body copy is tiny: a 9–10px column about 30% wide placed at bottom left.

## Colour
Each project gets its own acid colour, used at maximum saturation: fluoro green (#39FF14), safety yellow, magenta (#FF1F8F), cyan. These sit on photographic or black grounds. The website frame stays black on white.

## Imagery
- In-situ documentary photography with a flash or street feel: shop signage on a real street, banners on a Palladian façade, a man walking past a poster.
- Hands holding printed matter.
- Lightly styled food still-lifes.

## Case-study presentation
1. Hero: OOH or signage in situ.
2. Intro sentence.
3. Carousel of signage, shopfront and food photos.
4. Flat artwork: a calendar grid, a poster.
5. Mobile UI in hand.
6. Stationery or packaging, flat-lay pairs.
7. An 8-up mosaic of event, merch and hand shots.
8. Small long-copy block.
9. "Related project".

## Signature moves
- A wordmark stacked 3 lines deep that "hangs" from the top edge of each application.
- Fluoro type overprinted on documentary photos.
- One loud custom display face per client.
- Tiny bold captions under big images.
- Mosaics of 8 tight crops of hands and objects.
- Glitch or blur-gradient halos behind type (RA).
- Editorial rhythm: condensed caps headline over a full-bleed portrait.

## 3 notable projects
- **Royal Academy Schools Show (2023):** an "un-RA" exhibition identity. Fluorescent green and pink, a heavy-metal type, glitch motion, OOH banners on Burlington House and the AFTERTASTE catalogue.
- **Noble Rot magazine (ongoing, c.2023):** an editorial redesign with custom typefaces, screen-printed covers, and clashing serif/condensed spreads.
- **Ancestrel Wines (c.2024):** a stacked lurid wordmark loosely shaped like a bunch of grapes, hung from the top of every application. It appears on shopfronts, calendars, merch and stickers, in a 1970s-magazine palette.
