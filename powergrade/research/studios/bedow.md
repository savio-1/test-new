# Bedow (Stockholm)

URL: https://www.bedow.se. A strategic design and branding studio founded by Perniclas Bedow. It has been working "for over two decades".

## Philosophy
"We have built brands for good companies with good intentions. We use our naive curiosity, impactful narratives, and ingenious craft to change brands for good." There are five phases: Discover, Define, Imagine, Explore, Craft. The studio leaves room for intuition. "If there is no substance to our clients then we cannot invent it for them": design is "an extension of a product or service".

## Typography
- The site uses a custom family: **Bedow Text/Head** (a neutral grotesk) at 16px and 12px, and **Bedow Hand**, a calligraphic, humanist display at 36px/1.1 and about 87px. Hand is used for statements and intros.
- A waving-hand pictogram serves as the logo.
- Client work often gets a custom typeface or logotype:
  - Neko's angular segmented sans, with technical numerals in ultra-light;
  - Swee's wordmark drawn from oscillating yeast-like shapes;
  - Coop's friendly rounded caps.
- Data is set in small caps or monospace with orange highlights ("SCAPIS 2 MIND*").

## Layout & grid
- A white page with a 4% margin. Each project row has a year at 1%, the name and tagline at 5–20%, and an optional description at 55–75%.
- Images are asymmetric: big at left (about 57%) with a small one at right (about 40%). Some rows are offset to start at 15% or end at 77%.
- A coloured **Insight / Concept / Solution** band runs full-width, in navy for Polar and yellow (#FFF36D) for Neko. Three numbered columns of about 22% each, plus a small looped-arrow diagram.
- Long, image-heavy pages (about 20,000px) with captions in 9px.

## Colour
Each brand gets its own sophisticated palette:
- Polar Night (#172533) with Midnight Sun (#EAD9A7);
- Neko's sage, mint and blush pastels with coral data text;
- Swee's peach (#F5CBBF) and mint (#BFDCCB) with stripe-coded ingredients;
- Coop's bright, plate-based packaging colours.

## Imagery
- Pastel still-life photography with a single object and one or two props on a seamless backdrop.
- Clean packaging line-ups on white.
- Architectural interiors.
- Black-and-white portraiture.
- Motion loops of logos and data.

## Signature moves
- One modular "carrier" shape across thousands of SKUs (Coop's superellipse "plate").
- Stripe-coded infographic labels showing ingredient ratios.
- Generative or organic wordmarks.
- Pastel seamless still-lifes with a hero object and props.
- A big humanist-calligraphic statement face for narrative.
- A coloured three-column strategy band.
- Data typography in ultra-light numerals.

## 3 notable projects
- **Polar Music Prize (2021):** a redrawn crown/stave symbol, Polar Night and Midnight Sun colours, an animated five-line stave and a laureate website.
- **Neko Health (2023):** naming, tagline and a custom type for preventive body scanning. Dot-cloud imagery, pastel clinics and technical data typography.
- **Coop Nordic private label (2024):** 3,500 SKUs in four markets unified by the superellipse "plate" carrier.
