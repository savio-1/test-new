# CENTER (Brooklyn, NY)

URL: https://center.design. Confirmed as the brand design company founded in 2018 by Alex Center, formerly design director at Coca-Cola (vitaminwater, smartwater). It is based at 61 Greenpoint Ave, Brooklyn and runs the "Front Center" podcast. I reviewed the homepage and the United Sodas, Conbody, Kin Euphorics and New Balance Basketball case studies. The site blocks some browser user agents. Captures worked with a plain user agent.

## Philosophy
"CENTER is a brand design company based in Brooklyn, New York." The studio makes **challenger CPG and culture brands** that disrupt categories with one bold, simple idea: twelve flavours in twelve flat colours ("challenge the soda quo"), or prison-style grit for a fitness studio staffed by formerly incarcerated trainers. The work shows packaging-first thinking, merch as identity and press-worthy wit.

## Typography
- **House style:**
  - **Ginto** (Nord-style wide) at weight 800, uppercase, about 12.7% of width, -8.6px tracking for "CENTER";
  - **Suisse Int'l** for everything else. Nav labels are about 1.4% of width (20px) in bold, and statements are 22px bold and centred.
  - The splash is a giant black "©"-style C mark centred on white.
- **Client work:**
  - small, **widely tracked uppercase sans** (United Sodas' vertical can text);
  - stencil caps (Conbody);
  - mono-weight condensed caps;
  - spaced uppercase on stationery ("COSS MARTE / CEO/FOUNDER").

## Layout & grid
- The nav labels are **pinned to the four corners**: Work at top-left, Info at top-right, News at bottom-left, Podcast at bottom-right. On case pages they become a top row of 4 equal columns with the C mark centred.
- Case layout:
  - a full-bleed hero;
  - a **black band** with one centred statement (max 55% width);
  - a mosaic of images with **narrow black gutters (~0.6%)**: full-width, then 2-up, with occasional 1/3 + 2/3 splits;
  - a second centred statement in the black band mid-page;
  - team, services, awards and collaborator credits.

## Colour
- The house is pure black and white.
- Client colour is **flat, matte and multi-hue**: United Sodas' 12-colour pastel-to-primary can spectrum; Conbody's concrete grey, black and white; Kin's cosmic gradients.

## Signature moves
- One flat colour per SKU across a large range.
- Diagonal repeat product patterns.
- Vertical small-caps text running up the pack.
- The logo applied to a real surface (stencil on concrete) as the hero.
- Corner-pinned navigation.
- A black statement band between image runs.
- A heavy wide uppercase display wordmark.

## 3 notable projects
- **United Sodas of America (2020):** 12 matte flat-colour cans with vertical type and a variety pack. Won at Cannes Lions, ADC and Dieline.
- **Conbody:** prison-style fitness. A stencil wordmark, bar-tally symbol and grit-textured stationery.
- **New Balance Basketball (2019):** the campaign and identity for the category relaunch.
