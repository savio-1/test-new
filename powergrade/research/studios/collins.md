# COLLINS (New York / San Francisco)

URL: https://wearecollins.com. Founded in 2008 by Brian Collins and Leland Maschmeyer. The site lists "8x Agency of the Year" (AdAge 2019–2026 and D&AD 2021). Sources: the homepage and the Red Wing Shoes, Robinhood, San Francisco Symphony and Target up&up case pages, with the carousel images pulled from the DOM.

## Philosophy
- The tagline is "Rewrite your worth". COLLINS now sells "Programs" such as Brand Refresh, Reposition, Expansion and Turnaround, which it describes as ways "to find and command their unique premium".
- Design is framed as making a future "so irresistible, it becomes inevitable".
- The work tends to build a whole world around a brand: an illustrated city, a living typeface.

## Typography
- The site uses **Portrait Text** (Commercial Type), a sharp transitional serif, for headlines. The hero is 72px with −0.02em tracking, in sentence case. **Graphik** is used at 16px for UI.
- Client work leans on expressive custom type:
  - **SF Symphony**: *ABC Symphony*, a variable version of Dinamo's Arizona Serif. The letters stretch, skew and shorten in sequence, so "SFSYMPHONY" reads like a crescendo.
  - **Robinhood (2020)**: Capsule Sans with Nib serif.
  - **up&up**: huge rounded sans words ("Snack") set across the faces of the pack.

## Layout & grid
- Case pages use a near-centred column of about 600px.
- Each case opens with the title in serif at the 37% x-position and a one-line subtitle, then a horizontal carousel of rounded (8px) image cards that bleeds off the right edge.
- The metadata (Program, Industry, Stage) sits in a small left column.
- "Impact" is a row of white stat cards: a giant number at the top, a label and description at the bottom, and a fourth card cropped at the edge.

## Colour
- The site frame alternates between warm near-black (#140805) and off-white (#F7F6F2).
- Client palettes are saturated and ownable: Robinhood green and chartreuse, up&up orange and mint, Symphony black with jewel-tone posters.

## Imagery
- Commissioned illustration worlds. For Robinhood, isometric cities in green and yellow by several illustrators.
- Lush in-situ photography: posters on bus shelters, a wordmark carved into a stone facade, a person with a product in nature.
- **Process boards**: application mockups sit in white frames, with orange marker arrows, circles and handwritten notes scrawled over them.

## Case-study presentation
The usual order is: hero carousel (product macro, archive photo, mark construction) → narrative intro → "Augments" (system components shown as stacked cards) → Impact stats → Press logos → a related case.

## Signature moves
1. Hand annotations in orange marker over clean presentation boards.
2. Type that changes its own proportions: stretched, crescendo or elastic.
3. A single illustrated universe that carries the whole brand.
4. A hushed dark frame with one serif sentence in the centre.
5. Rounded image cards in horizontal carousels that bleed off the edge.
6. Results presented as giant-number stat cards.

## Notable projects
- **San Francisco Symphony (2021).** A variable serif that reacts to music. Street posters, carved signage and a wordmark with letters that shorten in sequence. TDC Best in Show.
- **Robinhood (2020).** A world of commissioned illustration, a bold icon set and information graphics. Capsule Sans with Nib.
- **Target up&up (c. 2025).** One flexible system across 2,000+ SKUs. Giant words on colour-coded packs.
