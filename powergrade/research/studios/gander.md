# Gander (Brooklyn, NY)

URL: https://takeagander.com. Confirmed as the branding and graphic design studio at 124 9th St, Brooklyn, founded around 2015. It specialises in branding, packaging, web design and art direction for CPG and culture brands. Fast Company named it one of the most innovative design companies of 2024. I reviewed the homepage, the work index, and the Graza, Celebrate Brooklyn and Smallhold case studies. The Yellowbird page failed to render (an app error).

## Philosophy
"Welcome to the Ganderverse. We're a team of earth-based creatives whose purpose is to unstick what's stuck, solve for x, and transform brilliant ideas into brands that can't be ignored."

The work is research-first. The Graza case opens with survey findings: fewer than 1% of shoppers knew what "extra virgin" means. The output is **joyful, illustrated and anti-pretentious**: "fancy oil in a decidedly un-fancy format". Gander also runs a side project called Make Meadows (community gardens).

## Typography
- **House style:** **Denim INK**, a friendly grotesk.
  - Nav is 11px uppercase.
  - Project titles are about 9% of width, regular weight, sentence case.
  - The intro is a 3-line statement at about 2.6% of width.
  - Section heads ("The Latest", "what we do") are set in **wild display lettering**: an inflated blackletter, a brush script and a 3D chrome script. A blackletter "The Gander Times" marquee scrolls across the page.
  - The logo is a giant soft-blob wordmark spanning the full width.
- **Client work:** custom wordmarks drawn from old-world letterforms (Graza), condensed festival caps (Celebrate Brooklyn) and retro soda scripts.

## Layout & grid
- A cream page (#FBF2EC). The margin is 2.8%, and media tiles have **small rounded corners (~6px)** with a 1.5% gap.
- Case header:
  - a full-width 16:9 hero film;
  - the title at about 9% of width, left;
  - the body text in a column from 13–55%;
  - a services list in small type from 72%.
- Media rhythm: full-width, then 2-up (50/50), then a full-width illustration sheet on cream, then 2-up again. Some tiles are **pure flat brand-colour blocks** used as rests.

## Colour
- The house is cream with a near-black (#171717) ink.
- Client palettes are **food-bright**: Graza's chartreuse (#D4E038) and olive-black (#2F3A2A); Celebrate Brooklyn's yellow, violet, orange, pink and cobalt.

## Signature moves
- One-ink character illustration libraries.
- Flat colour blocks as tiles in the image grid.
- A quilt or patchwork geometric pattern system.
- Mixing wild display lettering with a calm grotesk.
- Rounded image tiles on cream.
- A giant full-bleed wordmark in the footer.
- Product shot with everyday props (sneakers, grocery bags).

## 3 notable projects
- **Graza (2022):** a squeezy-bottle olive oil. Old-world custom wordmark, chartreuse and olive, mascot illustrations.
- **Celebrate Brooklyn (2023):** a festival identity built on quilting ("gathering"), with patchwork geometry across banners and the Bandshell.
- **Smallhold (mushroom pesto):** a mushroom-stem-shaped tub that makes the ingredient the hero of the pack.
