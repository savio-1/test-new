# Inhouse (Auckland)

URL: https://inhousedesign.co.nz. The site says "Celebrating 30 Years" and gives the studio's address as Hinemoa Street, Birkenhead. Credits for The Hotel Britomart name Arch MacDonnell (creative director) and Toby Curnow (design director). Sources: the homepage ("Fragments from 30 years Inhouse"), the Index and Grid views, and 16 lightbox project frames (NZ Post, Hotel Britomart, NZIFF, Karen Walker, Ossa, Grava, Lion, Auckland University Press and others).

## Philosophy
- The studio's own site is an archive, not a sales pitch. There are no case-study essays, only "fragments": single artefacts with five facts each (name, client, discipline, sector, year).
- The work is craft-led and culturally rooted in Aotearoa: books, wine, art institutions, public services, hospitality. Typography and objecthood come first.

## Typography
- The site is set only in **Helvetica**: 17.5px, +0.015em tracking, regular weight, sentence case.
- Client work ranges widely:
  - strict geometric marks: the NZ Post chevron, Ossa letters in squares, Karen Walker's "M" made of arrows;
  - book typography;
  - hand-drawn lettering commissions with artists such as John Reynolds.
- Wordmarks are often constructed from simple modules: squares, circles, arrows.

## Layout & grid
- The homepage is one fragment per screen: a single image of 16–25% of width, floating at different x-positions on pure white, sometimes with 1–2 sibling images stacked behind it with a slight offset.
- The 7 navigation items are spread evenly across the top edge.
- The Index view is a 5-column table with hairline rules.
- The Grid view is a 9-across greyscale contact sheet.
- The lightbox shows the image at 95% of width with "Close" centred above, vertical "Prev"/"Next" at the edges, and the 5-field caption row along the bottom.

## Colour
- Neutral and material: white, black, kraft, concrete.
- When colour arrives it is a single strong field: NZ Post red and navy, NZIFF deep red or blue gradient fields, Grava's orange dots, Steinlager red.

## Imagery
- Moody in-situ photography: pictogram signs on raw concrete, a truck in a yard, a tote carried through a lobby.
- Product still-life on plinths.
- Book spreads shot overhead.
- Studio "incidental" photos.
- Many images are shown in greyscale in the grid.

## Case-study presentation
- No narrative. There are atomised artefacts instead: logo, then livery, then signage, then posters, then merchandise, each as its own captioned fragment.
- In-situ shots far outnumber flat mockups.

## Signature moves
1. One artefact per screen in vast white space.
2. Five-field factual captions in place of prose.
3. Marks built from squares or windows scattered like a constellation.
4. Pictogram signage in thin gold line on black squares.
5. Big diagonal light-ray gradients for festival posters.
6. Greyscale contact-sheet indexes.

## Notable projects
- **The Hotel Britomart (2021).** A mark made from an array of squares taken from the facade's windows. Wayfinding, posters and tote. Best Awards Gold Pins for identity and signage.
- **NZ Post (2021).** A master logo and a bold red chevron livery on the truck fleet, plus brand photography.
- **Ossa Wines (2022–23).** A logo set of O-S-S-A letters in squares that rearrange, plus tissue paper and packaging.
