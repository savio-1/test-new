# Jones Knowles Ritchie / JKR (London / New York)

URL: https://www.jkrglobal.com. Founded in 1990 as an independent brand design agency, with offices in London, New York, Singapore and Shanghai. I reviewed the homepage, the work index, and the Burger King, Mozilla, Yahoo and RSPCA case studies.

## Philosophy
"If you remember only one thing about us, it's **Be Distinctive Everywhere**." "Since 1990, we've helped brands be who they are, only more so." JKR believes in **distinctive brand assets**: own a colour, a shape, a typeface and a tone, then repeat them relentlessly across packaging, retail, uniforms and social. Its signature is **retro-revival** (Burger King, Yahoo, Manischewitz) and witty, appetite-driven craft. Effectiveness claims sit up front ("stock jumped 7.8%").

## Typography
- **House style:** the custom **JKRRitchie**, a light grotesk used at 64px with 1.4 line-height for manifesto text. Scroll-revealed text fades from 25% grey to white as you read.
- **JKRKnowles** is used for UI. The lowercase "jkr" monogram is in a serif.
- **Client work:** custom display faces are the hero:
  - Burger King's "Flame", soft and chunky;
  - Mozilla's uniwidth "Mozilla Sans/Slab";
  - Yahoo's custom serif.
- Words are set as **pattern and package**: "GOOEY" repeated as wallpaper, flavour names warped onto wrappers.

## Layout & grid
- A dark page (#111).
- "WORK" and "MENU" sit as tiny labels pinned to the **far left and right edges, vertically centred**. The jkr monogram is top-centre.
- The case column is **centred at about 78% width** (11% side margins). The intro is left-aligned at about 45% width: a 64px headline and two paragraphs.
- Media stack in a single column with about 0.6% (8px) gaps:
  - 16:9 hero films;
  - full-column photographs;
  - occasional 2-up type specimens.
- Footer: "Find / Follow / Connect" stacked in huge light type, with the last two words in grey.

## Colour
- The house is charcoal (#111) and white.
- Client work is **saturated and appetite-led**: Burger King's flame orange, BBQ brown and mustard; Mozilla's black, green and white; Yahoo's purple.

## Signature moves
- One owned word repeated as an all-over type pattern.
- A custom chunky display face warped onto packs.
- Retro-revival of a heritage mark.
- Brand-colour seamless sweeps for products and uniforms.
- A billboard with one giant onomatopoeic word ("MMMM").
- Scroll-to-reveal manifesto text.
- Centred single-column case scroll on near-black.

## 3 notable projects
- **Burger King (2021):** the 1969–99 bun logo revived, the "Flame" typeface, a food-colour palette and uniforms.
- **Mozilla (2024):** "Hear Mozilla Roar". A pixel-dino flag symbol, uniwidth Mozilla Sans/Slab and a black-and-green palette.
- **RSPCA (2024):** "It Takes a Society", a repositioning for the charity's next 200 years.
