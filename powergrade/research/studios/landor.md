# Landor (San Francisco / global)

URL: https://landor.com. A WPP brand consultancy founded in 1941 by Walter Landor. It was Landor & Fitch from 2019 to 2023 and rebranded as Landor in November 2023. **Note:** landor.com refused or timed out for both the headless browser and the fetch tools in this session, so I could not take screenshots. This profile is built from secondary sources (It's Nice That, Creative Bloq, BP&O, iF and Red Dot, the VW Group press release, Landor's case-page metadata found through search) and established knowledge of the work.

## Philosophy
Landor calls itself a "brand transformation company". It works **strategy-to-experience**: consulting, identity, verbal, sonic, motion, packaging and spaces. CCO Teemu Suviala: "Today, brands are – by their very nature – omnipresent." Case studies lead with **business proof**:
- "+10% stock value within two days" (Syensqo);
- "56.9% increase in subscriptions" (Milan Symphony Orchestra);
- Simstore purchase-rate lifts (Kellogg's).

## Typography
- **House style:** a **serif wordmark** ("to differentiate from the overpopulated world of sans serif logotypes") paired with **Landor Sans**, an elegant grotesque from Landor's in-house type studio in Milan.
- **Client work:** custom typefaces are a core deliverable:
  - "The Group" for VW Group, a neo-grotesk for 200+ languages with rounded O and G;
  - TUMB TUMB for the Milan Symphony Orchestra;
  - custom industrial type for West Loop.
- Headlines are large, sentence case and tightly set.

## Layout & grid
- Case pages open with paired **landscape (16:9) and portrait (2:3) hero cuts** of the same key visual for responsive use.
- Then:
  - a short claim and context column;
  - full-bleed films;
  - 2- and 3-up application rows;
  - a **results band** of large numerals;
  - awards and credits.
- Generous white space, typically a 12-column grid with about 5% margins.

## Colour
- **House:** **ultramarine** (a nod to Walter Landor founding the firm on a ferry in San Francisco Bay) with white and black.
- **Client work:** systematised palettes, often with **moving gradients** (VW Group) or heritage-derived hues (West Loop's subway-line and brick colours).

## Imagery
- CGI and 3D product renders.
- Big in-situ signage (corporate HQs, cards, ATMs).
- **Bird's-eye "hyper-vision" photography** (BANCOMAT).
- Character illustration (nib, by Sebastian Curi).
- Motion loops users can draw (West Loop).

## Signature moves
- A bespoke typeface as the hero of the system.
- Gradient fields in motion behind a simple mark.
- A symbol derived from a historic or brand story (Syensqo's 24 dots from the 1911 Solvay Conference; BANCOMAT's seagull "B").
- A results band with outsized numerals.
- Paired landscape and portrait key visuals.
- Ultramarine as a house signal.

## 3 notable projects
- **Volkswagen Group (2024):** the "Progressive Movement" idea, "The Group" typeface (with Studio René Bieder), moving gradients and CGI. iF Gold 2025.
- **West Loop, Chicago (2024):** "Into the Loop". Draggable motion loops in heritage colours with custom industrial type.
- **BANCOMAT:** a seagull "B" symbol with a bird's-eye "hyper-vision" visual system.
