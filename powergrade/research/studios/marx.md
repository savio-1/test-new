# Marx Design (Auckland)

URL: https://marxdesign.co.nz. An "independent design and brand agency… designing identities, creative systems and packaging for lasting recognition, connection and growth", serving Australia, New Zealand and beyond. Sources: the homepage and the StrangeLove, Hunter's, Phoenix Organics and Autex Acoustics pages.

## Philosophy
- Marx works mainly in food, beverage, wine and spirits, beauty and the built environment.
- The method is research into the brand's origin, followed by a named idea. For StrangeLove that idea was "premium abstraction": soda reframed as an apothecary tonic, with 1960s–80s pop nostalgia.
- Packaging form, copywriting and photography art direction are done in-house. Almost every project credits photographer Yuki Sato.

## Typography
- The site is set in **Die Grotesk B Medium**. Body is 19px. The intro is 38px, with no tracking adjustments.
- Case titles are huge (about 4% of width), fixed top-left, and end with a trailing comma ("StrangeLove,", "Hunter's,").
- Client typography:
  - clean neo-grotesks, set with "unexpected typesetting": short ragged lines with odd indents, around playful copy;
  - refined Roman caps and foil crests for heritage wine;
  - corporate sans plus a pattern for industrial brands.

## Layout & grid
- The page background takes on the project's colour: white, cream or black.
- An asymmetric two-column flow:
  - the hero spans 6.5–95.5% of width;
  - a small services list (about 0.7% of width, in grey) sits at the far left;
  - the headline and two body columns start at 44%.
- Images stagger: a right-shifted image at 26–94%, then a half-width image at 5–49%, then a full 5–94% image. The gaps between groups are about 15% of the viewport.

## Colour
Each project owns its colour world:
- StrangeLove: cream with flavour blobs in lemon, sky, orange and red;
- Hunter's: deep olive and black with gold foil;
- Autex: strict black and off-white;
- Liquor Lickers: cobalt.

## Imagery
- Studio still-life with hard directional light.
- Bottles laid diagonally over coloured paper shapes.
- Faces covered by flavour shapes.
- Hands entering the frame.
- Heritage archive photography for wine.
- Debossing and foil detail macros.

## Case-study presentation
The usual order is: hero pack or poster → strategy text → poster series in situ on concrete → product family group shot → single-pack hero on a colour blob → campaign posters → brand guideline sheet → pattern panels → business cards or iPad → architectural application.

## Signature moves
1. Project names with a trailing comma, set huge.
2. An organic colour blob behind a cut-out product.
3. Ragged short-line headlines with unusual indents.
4. Product-derived patterns, such as tapered acoustic slats.
5. Tonal debossing: wordmark in the ground colour, read only by its shadow.
6. Page colour that changes per project.

## Notable projects
- **StrangeLove Lo-Cal Soda (2019).** "Premium abstraction": flavour blobs, a dot pattern and irreverent copy. Sales doubled after launch.
- **Hunter's Wines (c. 2024).** The clan crest redrawn as an oval, a wordmark with Celtic nuances, and MiruMiru sparkling packaging in olive and gold.
- **Autex Acoustics.** A customer-facing brand in black and white, with patterns drawn from acoustic panels. Guidelines, stationery and digital.
