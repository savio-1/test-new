# Monday Nights (London / Bristol)

URL: https://mondaynights.co. Verified as a real design studio: "Digital-first branding and design studio based in London and Bristol, creating distinctive brands for sports, tech and culture." Founded in 2023 by Matt Caldwell and Dan Allan (co-founders and creative directors). The team spans graphic, motion, 3D and type design. Headless Chromium got "upstream request failed" from the proxy for this host. The pages and all case-study media (stills plus first frames of the MP4s) were therefore fetched with curl, in page order, for Bolder Creative, Echospace, World Snooker Tour and The Red Bull Review.

## Philosophy
- "We make world-class design accessible."
- Their principles are Always Agile, Meaningful Partnerships, Raw Invention ("never stifle creativity with constraints") and Say it Simple.
- Clients include World Snooker Tour, Red Bull, SEGA, Aston Villa, Zwift and F1 Arcade.

## Typography
- The site uses **Apris** (Regular and Medium).
- Client work mixes refined and aggressive type:
  - a sculptural serif wordmark with wide-tracked serif caps for Bolder;
  - a custom techno-grotesk ("Echo Sans") with mono-like numerals for Echospace;
  - condensed sports caps with cut, stencil-like strokes ("BATTLE ON THE BAIZE") for WST;
  - ultra-condensed black display rotated vertically, with an italic condensed serif, for the Red Bull Review.
- Their own wordmark is a heavy grotesk "Monday Nights" stacked under a sawtooth "M".

## Layout & grid
- Case studies are built from two exact formats:
  - 3840×2160 (16:9) full-width plates;
  - 1920×2160 (8:9) half plates that pair into a 16:9 row.
- Their systems expose structure. "The Bolder Grid" is a deliberately visible guide grid with crop marks and corner dots, and it stays in finished social and web layouts.

## Colour
- Stripped-back neutrals (warm grey #E4E2DE, near-black) with one electric accent: Bolder lime #E1FF2E, WST red #E8102A, Echospace coral and cobalt.
- The Red Bull Review uses cool blue-grey studio light.

## Imagery
- Heavy 3D: a rotating extruded "B", hard hats, gilets and the logo as an object.
- Cut-out athletes framed by brand shapes.
- Editorial studio shoots: skaters and riders holding the printed magazine.
- Construction drawings with coordinates and annotations.

## Case-study presentation
The usual order is: logo reveal motion → wordmark construction (outlines with coordinates) → 3D symbol → lockups with guides → OOH digital boards 3-up → phones 3-up → social post and deck slide (halves) → type specimen → website scroll → merchandise (tee, tote, kit) → brand line.

## Signature moves
1. The logo as a looping 3D object. The static mark is one off-axis frame.
2. Visible grids, crop marks and corner dots kept in the final artwork.
3. A single neon accent used as a highlight bar behind text.
4. Construction plates with coordinate labels.
5. A brand shape used as a crop frame around cut-out people.
6. Strict 16:9 / 8:9 plate pairs.

## Notable projects
- **Bolder Creative (2026).** A rotating sculpted "B", a sculptural wordmark, a lime accent and the Bolder Grid.
- **World Snooker Tour (2024).** A triangle-rack symbol, event lockups, posters, app, broadcast graphics and kit.
- **The Red Bull Review.** A printed review of a year of press: a condensed vertical type masthead, shot in use by athletes.
