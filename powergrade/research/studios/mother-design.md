# Mother Design (London / New York / Los Angeles)

URL: https://www.motherdesign.com. This is the design arm of the independent agency Mother, with studios in London, Brooklyn and LA.

## Philosophy
"We move brands and ideas forward by designing new worlds in five senses and four dimensions. And we push, as mothers might, our partners… to be the truest… versions of themselves." Its four values are **Rigor, Rebellion, Depth, Care**. The design arm is strategy-led and willing to be maximalist: each case opens with the business question ("How do you design a community foundation into an iconic institution?"). Results are reported with numbers.

## Typography
- The site uses **Basis Grotesque** (Colophon), with **Basis Mono** in uppercase and +0.06em tracking for labels and metadata.
- Body text is 16px/1.2 at −0.02em. Statements are 48px with tight −0.04em tracking and a line height of 1.1. Display is 110px/600 at −0.04em.
- Client work swings widely: a heavy condensed slab for Brooklyn Org, a liquid custom serif for Rollr, a folk-illustrated wordmark for Keen. The site itself stays neutral.

## Layout & grid
- The site has a strict **12-column ruled grid**: hairline 1px rules divide sections into labelled cells (a 2 × 2 grid of values, each cell 50% wide).
- Case pages use a label column of about 0–25% (a small bold label such as "What we did", "Industries") and a text column of about 25–60%.
- Images come in three arrangements:
  - full-width (100% minus a 16px margin);
  - 2-up asymmetric (60/40 or 48/48 with a deliberate gap);
  - carousels with a "1/6 Previous / Next" caption bar.
- Big pull quotes run at 48px across about 65% of the width.
- Dark-mode case pages: the page background switches to near-black (#141414) for some projects.

## Colour
The site is neutral: white, #F3F3F3 and black. Each project owns a single dominant brand colour, shown as a full-bleed field: Keen's red (#9B0E0A), Rollr's burgundy (#4A0D2E) with pastel accents, Brooklyn Org's coral and sky gradients.

## Imagery

- Event and stage photography.

## Signature moves
- A strategic question as the case title.
- Ruled grid cells with tiny mono labels.
- A full-bleed single-colour field with a centred wordmark.
- Huge custom wordmarks cropped by the frame (BKO, HEARTLAND).
- A colour/texture triptych of swatch bars over macro photos.
- Results numbers set as large statements.
- Abbreviated or monogram brand marks: a circular "BKO" badge set into a marquee line.

## 3 notable projects
- **Brooklyn Org (2023):** renamed and rebranded Brooklyn Community Foundation. A heavy condensed wordmark, the BKO monogram, a "Foundation of our Future" marquee, totes and the Changemakers Ball stage.
- **Rollr (c.2024):** "conscious hedonism" for a refillable deodorant. A sculpted glass bottle, a liquid custom serif wordmark, burgundy plus pastels, and a "Rhapsody" chat UI.
- **Keen (2026):** recast a psychic platform as mass-wellness. A red brand field, a folk-art illustrated world, merch, and an immersive environment.
