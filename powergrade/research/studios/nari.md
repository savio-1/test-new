# Studio Nari (London)

URL: https://studionari.com (styled STUDIO NĀRĪ). Founded and run by Caterina Bianchini, previously Caterina Bianchini Studio and relaunched as Nari in February 2020. Based at Netil House, Hackney. **The site could not be screenshotted**: it serves a "Confirm you are human" bot challenge to headless browsers. The work was studied from press case studies instead:
- It's Nice That on Modular by Mensah;
- BP&O on 3TEMP;
- the Pangram Pangram interview on Mob, HOME, Nike LA "Legacy", Vogue Singapore and Charli XCX.

## Philosophy
- Nari bridges "design and art" and calls its approach "humanistic, artistic", combining artistic practice with strategic and psychological branding theory.
- Bianchini: "it's your duty as a designer to test and experiment."
- The work balances play with serious craft and hides small details that reward a second look.

## Typography
Type is the core material. The studio draws bespoke faces for most clients:
- *Modular* for Modular by Mensah: ultra-condensed black caps that mix sharp and body-like rounded terminals, with letters swapped for solid dots;
- liquid, ligatured serif and script wordmarks for Nike LA "Legacy", explored in dozens of variants;
- variable Right Grotesk for Mob.

Headlines are set very large and very tight, often filling the width.

## Layout & grid
- Type behaves "like furniture": it anchors the top or bottom edge of a composition and "dresses the page the way furniture dresses a room".
- A typical poster has a headline filling the top fifth and a small centred object photo floating in a flat grey or colour field.
- Split canvases: a typographic manifesto on one half, the monogram on the other.

## Colour
- Usually one hot accent against warm neutrals: Mensah orange with stone grey; 3TEMP terracotta, brown, deep red and a flash of mint.
- Campaign work goes fully saturated: red, cobalt, ultraviolet.

## Imagery
- Sculptural product photography on plinths.
- Wheat-paste posters and billboards in situ (Shoreditch walls, HOME hoarding).
- Chrome or 3D-lit type renders.
- Sticker-bomb collage for Mob.
- Debossed leather, metal and paper macros for 3TEMP.

## Case-study presentation
The usual order is: wordmark on colour → poster series → OOH in situ → social screens (3 phones) → tote and merchandise → editorial spread → shipping container or exhibition → pattern.

## Signature moves
1. A bespoke display face for every brand.
2. Letters replaced by dots or circles inside headlines.
3. A headline as a full-width band at the top, with an object floating below.
4. Liquid or ligatured wordmarks rendered as chrome.
5. One hot colour against stone grey.
6. A modular logo whose construction drives the layout grid (3TEMP).
7. Monograms used as embossed objects: key fobs, spoons, ice cubes.

## Notable projects
- **Modular by Mensah (2021).** A custom "Modular" typeface and a slotted MBM monogram. Posters, tote, social and a container.
- **Mob (2021).** A rebrand rolled out over ten months: a flexible Right Grotesk system with sticker and illustration energy.
- **3TEMP (2026).** The existing modular wordmark extended into a layout system: embossing, packaging, trade-show booth, signage and merchandise.
