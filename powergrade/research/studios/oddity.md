# .Oddity Studio (Hong Kong)

URL: https://odditystudio.com. **Verified** as the Hong Kong studio founded in 2018 by Alice Mourou, with Kirill Runkov and Nikita Shchukin. It has won D&AD (Graphite and Wood Pencils), Tokyo TDC and Dezeen awards. It also runs its own perfume house, Oddity Fragrance. Do not confuse it with Oddity Form (LA interiors) or the Prague bar named Oddity.

## Philosophy
"Practicing design as conceptual art." Concept is "a non-negotiable part of the design process… the filter for decisions." The studio rejects "useless rules and templates" and tests ideas physically: stamping with vegetables, writing with closed eyes. It treats a layout like a story, with setup, climax and resolution.

## Typography
- The site is set in a custom **Oddity Typold Condensed** (Light 300 / Book / Black) at 16px/1.15, plus 43px for the project index.
- A signature quirk: **dotless i's** throughout ("Perfumes as self-reflectıon").
- Client faces are concept-driven:
  - a custom "haunted hand" script for Dead Poets, written blind and off-hand;
  - mirrored and inverted ES Build for nown unown;
  - a small, technical grotesk for perfume labels.
- Type is often reflected, rotated 180° or set vertically.

## Layout & grid
- The work index is a pure type list in 43px light. Each line is a poetic title ("Haunted Hand", "Beauty of imperfections") indented to one of 5 tab stops at 0 / 17 / 33 / 45 / 50% of the width. The pattern shifts on reload.
- Fixed corner furniture: ".Oddity" bottom left, "Studio" bottom right, navigation top left, email top right, all small.
- Case pages: a 2-column header (bold name plus a light poetic subtitle at about 38%), then body text indented to about 12%. Images sit on #EEEEEE plates in a 2-up 50/50 grid with 8px gutters and full-width rows. Grids of many small tiles show variation, such as 60 logo versions or 21 resin caps.

## Colour
Mostly white, light grey (#EEE) plates and black. Colour appears only as material: walnut, resin, a single signal red (#E5231B) for Dead Poets menus, cream paper.

## Imagery
- Tactile object photography on seamless grey: resin bottle caps, debossed boxes.
- Darkly lit interiors.
- X-ray-like photos of ingredient slices.
- Process photos and many-up variation grids.

## Case-study presentation
1. Hero logo or object on grey.
2. Name, poetic subtitle and services.
3. Indented narrative.
4. Logo transformation sequence (vertical arrows).
5. Interior or shopfront.
6. Packaging on grey.
7. 60-up grid of variants.
8. Social posts on black.
9. Printed matter on red.
10. Credits: "Typeface:", "Paper:", "Featured on:".

## Signature moves
- A concept-generated logo that is never fixed (haunted-hand variants).
- Mirrored or inverted wordmarks.
- Dotless or modified glyphs as a house tic.
- Many-up variation grids showing the system's range.
- Objects on seamless #EEE plates with no props.
- An index of poetic one-liners in place of client names.
- Credits including paper stock and typeface.

## 3 notable projects
- **Oddity Fragrance (2021–; Dezeen 2024 shortlist):** "beauty of imperfections". Hand-cast resin and wood caps, each one unique, a clinical label system and an impression-map leaflet.
- **Dead Poets (c.2024; D&AD 2025):** a dive bar and barbershop. A logo "written by ghosts" with a haunted hand, a custom type and red menus.
- **nown unown (c.2025):** a Korean niche perfume built on a mirror idea. Reflected typography, an inverted wordmark, subtly distorted images and the Seoul showroom.
