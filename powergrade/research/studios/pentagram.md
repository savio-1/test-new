# Pentagram (London / New York / Austin / Berlin)

URL: https://www.pentagram.com. 23 partners. Each partner runs their own team, and they work together "in friendship". The firm says it is "the only major design studio" where the owners are also the people who make the work. Sources: the homepage and the Folger Shakespeare Library, Natural History Museum, Guggenheim and Hiut pages, screenshotted in October 2026.

## Philosophy
Pentagram calls itself a "multi-disciplinary, independently owned" consultancy whose tagline is "We design Everything for Everyone". Every identity comes from one idea that can be stated in a sentence, for example "one brand, one constellation" for the Guggenheim. That idea is then carried out across every touchpoint with almost no decoration.

## Typography
- The site is set entirely in Optimo's **Plain**. Body text is 19px. Case-study titles are 52px with −0.02em tracking, in sentence case.
- In client work the type is usually custom or a customised version of a known face:
  - **Folger**: *Puck Headline* (with Tobias Frere-Jones, based on Mallory), *Mallory* and *Practice*.
  - **Natural History Museum**: *NHM Wallop*.
  - **Guggenheim**: *Guggenheim Sans* (based on Inter), with Playfair as the secondary face.
  - **Hiut**: Founders Grotesk Medium and Semibold. Uppercase titling is tracked at 15/60.
- Display type is often a stack of uppercase lines, each sized to fill the same measure, like a playbill.

## Layout & grid
- Case pages have a 20px (1.4%) outer margin and 10px gutters.
- Images run in a strict rhythm: full-width 16:10 slides, then 50/50 pairs. In a pair, one tall image on one side often sits against two stacked 4:3 images on the other.
- Pull quotes start at the 50% column. There is very little body copy; it is hidden behind an "About the project +" toggle.

## Colour
- Each identity gets one signature colour against neutrals. The Folger uses a red-orange on black and paper white. The Guggenheim is black on warm off-white, with yellow as an accent. The NHM is the exception: a broad three-colour sunburst palette on dark grounds.
- The website itself is white, with charcoal (#222) sections.

## Imagery
- Architectural photography of signage in situ: stone, concrete, brushed steel.
- Flat graphic slides of the mark on a solid ground.
- Short motion loops.
- Merchandise shot as tight 2×2 grids with no gutters: embossed paper, cup, tote, label.

## Case-study presentation
The usual order is: mark on a solid ground → heritage or context photos → typography slide → pull quote → signage in situ → environmental views → posters or banners → digital → merchandise grid.

## Signature moves
1. A single letter or initial cropped huge, so that it becomes the whole symbol.
2. Stacked uppercase lines that each fill one measure.
3. The wordmark sits on hairline rules that run through it.
4. One colour against black or off-white. Nothing else.
5. Pull quotes indented to the centre column, in large text.
6. A 2×2 merchandise grid with no gutters.
7. A generative symbol, such as word rings or ripples, that moves through four set motions.

## Notable projects
- **Folger Shakespeare Library (2024, Abbott Miller).** A red-orange "F" isolated from Puck Headline. Faceted silver lettering at the entrances, and garden inscriptions.
- **Natural History Museum (2024, Marina Willer with Nomad).** An "NHM" ripple sunburst and Word Rings. A code-based generator and four motion routes. Banners, posters, tote and tee.
- **Guggenheim (c. 2025, Harry Pearce).** A single-tier system for four museums. A "G" symbol, Guggenheim Sans and a modular layout framework.
