# Perky Bros (Nashville)

URL: https://perkybros.com. Founded in 2009 by Jeff (Jefferson) Perky; its tagline is "Est. 1883 & 2009". It does naming, strategy and identity for hospitality, retail, consumer and finance clients.

## Philosophy
"A branding and design team here to help ambitious companies challenge conventions and become undeniable in their space… tailored solutions rooted in insight, creativity, and delightful craft." "We don't repeat ourselves or subscribe to a set design style." The work has a warm, crafted Americana feel, made modern: arts-and-crafts marks, sporting nostalgia and southern hospitality, all refined.

## Typography
- The site pairs **Quadrant Text** (a bookish serif) at 60px/1.13, −0.7px, with **Dada Grotesk Medium** in uppercase at 16–18px for labels and navigation.
- Intro paragraphs use a **first-line indent** of about 12% of the column, like a novel.
- In client work:
  - typewriter-slab and serif wordmarks ("Have A Nice Game™" with a ™);
  - wide-tracked monospace caps for Foxtrot ("WE'LL BRING IT TO YOU!" with letters bouncing on the baseline);
  - spaced Roman capitals for Southall;
  - custom illustrated marks.

## Layout & grid
- A 2-column feed: each image tile is about 48% wide, captioned below with the CLIENT in uppercase grotesk at left and the tagline in serif at about 18%. A counter ("05 of 06") sits at right.
- The site background is cream (#F5F1E7); type is #1E1F20.
- Case page:
  - hero 100% wide;
  - a label row;
  - a large serif intro paragraph (about 30px on a 1440px page);
  - an "APPROACH:" label column (0–15%) with 11px serif body at 17–48% and an image at 50–100%.
- Then full-bleed 2-up and 1-up photos with a 16px gutter. **The page background takes the project's colour**: Southall clay (#9C7A68), Foxtrot black, HANG warm grey.

## Colour
Earthy, warm and tactile: clay, terracotta (#B5512E), sage (#8E9C80), navy (#1F2F55), kraft, cream. Brights such as Foxtrot's yellow and red are rare and used as punctuation.

## Imagery
- Tactile in-situ photography: letterpress cards with torn edges under raking light, embroidered caps, woven labels, kraft bags.
- Farm and lifestyle documentary photos.
- Products in seasonal natural settings, like a book in autumn leaves.

## Signature moves
- Background colour switches per case.
- Serif intro with a first-line indent.
- A caption pairing of UPPERCASE grotesk client name and serif tagline.
- Deboss, letterpress and torn deckle edges shown under hard raking shadow.
- A friendly mascot or gesture icon (thumbs-up, rocket).
- Products in the landscape: autumn leaves, orchard, pool.
- Words stacked as product (FOX / TROT boxes).

## 3 notable projects
- **Southall (c.2025):** naming and identity for a Tennessee farm resort. An arts-and-crafts "table" mark with three diamonds, letterpress in clay and sage, and embossed SOUTHALL.
- **Have A Nice Game (HANG):** 1980s–90s leisure sportswear. A thumbs-up symbol, a typewriter-serif wordmark and pennants.
- **Foxtrot:** custom type, illustration and packaging for a Chicago corner store and delivery market. Monospaced bouncing caps and an icon tissue pattern.
