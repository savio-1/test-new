# Regular Practice (London)

URL: https://regularpractice.co.uk. The `.com` domain in the brief is a parked GoDaddy page. Founded by Tom Finn and Kristoffer Sølling, who met at the RCA (2016–17), at 186 Homerton High Street, E9. It's Nice That "Ones to Watch" 2019. Sources: the homepage and the Tom Dixon, Purplebricks, Future Snoops and Form pages. These are inner-scroll modals, scrolled programmatically. Video frames came from Mux thumbnails.

## Philosophy
- The studio describes its approach as "playful yet carefully considered", and its sector stance as "Sector-agnostic by choice. Category-defining by practice."
- Its principles include "Fresh satisfies" and "Details compound".
- Clients include Adidas, Acne Studios, Uniqlo, Fred Perry, Tom Dixon, Serpentine, Design Museum, Somerset House, Octopus Energy and Purplebricks.

## Typography
- The site is set in **Antarctica**:
  - 12px/380 labels with +0.005em tracking;
  - about 30px light headlines in sentence case ("New ideas. New approaches.").
- Client type is commercial and confident:
  - Oatmeal Semibold and Medium for Purplebricks, uppercase on tape and signage;
  - a tight black grotesk stacked monogram ("FS") for Future Snoops;
  - a foundry-stamp hexagon monogram for Form.
- Project titles are written as one-line taglines rather than client names ("Designed in London, forged in Sheffield").

## Layout & grid
- The homepage opens with a big portrait (3:4.6) rounded-corner video card. A small thumbnail and caption sit inside the card's top-left corner, with a "VIEW" pill.
- Next comes a "Latest updates" row of small tagged cards (FEATURE / LAUNCH), then "Our Work" as a 4-up tile row.
- Projects open as a bottom sheet with a drag-handle pill and a centred title. The content alternates:
  - full-bleed 4:3 frames;
  - 50/50 pairs with about 6px gutters;
  - very large white gaps of about 25% of the viewport.

## Colour
- One saturated brand colour per client, applied physically: Purplebricks purple, Future Snoops acid chartreuse (#D9DD10), Form brick red (#9A2A0C).
- The site itself is off-white #FAFAFA.

## Imagery
- Mostly motion.
- Tight physical macros: tape on a cardboard box, a stamped blade, knife in lemons.
- Signage in real streets, such as a house-shaped estate-agent board in front of a brick terrace.
- Hands with print.
- Silhouetted products on dark gradients and 3D product renders.

## Case-study presentation
The usual order is: motion hero (logo on brand colour) → product or packaging macro pair → in-situ signage → print pair (brochure, welcome pack) → website on device → lifestyle.

## Signature moves
1. The brand colour as material: tape, boards, stamped steel.
2. 50/50 macro pairs, with an object on one side and colour on the other.
3. Huge white gaps between image groups.
4. Taglines in place of project titles.
5. Rounded portrait video cards with an inset caption.
6. Stamped or embossed monograms in hexagons or lozenges.

## Notable projects
- **Purplebricks (2023, with Sonder & Tell).** "Own it" / "No Bull". Oatmeal type, house-shaped boards, purple tape and a welcome pack.
- **Form.** Cookware "designed in London, forged in Sheffield", with a foundry-stamp monogram on blades and red boxes.
- **Future Snoops.** A trend-agency identity: a stacked FS monogram on acid yellow and a digital-first platform.
