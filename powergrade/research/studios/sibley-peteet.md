## Philosophy
Sibley/Peteet Design was founded in Dallas in 1982 by Don Sibley and Rex Peteet, both alumni of The Richards Group. An Austin office followed in 1994, and in 2010 Peteet merged his practice into Sherry Matthews Group as Studio/Peteet. It belongs to the **1980s–2000s "Texas design" generation** with Pirtle, Woody and Richards. That generation was known for **idea-driven, illustrative, craft-heavy graphic design**: witty visual puns, hand-drawn marks, award-winning annual reports and paper promotions. Its awards include NY ADC, LA ADC, the AR 100 and Communication Arts. Peteet is an AIGA Fellow (2015) and won the DSVC Golden Egg (2005), and his posters are in the Library of Congress and Poster House.

## Typography
- Classic book serifs (Garamond, Caslon, Bodoni) set with generous letterspacing in small caps.
- **Hand-lettered** titles and scripts.
- Combinations of an all-caps serif and an italic serif.
- Type often integrated into illustration (the word as image).

## Layout & grid
- Print-first and centred.
- **Posters:** one big illustrative idea filling 60–70% of the sheet, with a short title and credits in small caps at the foot.
- **Annual reports and paper promotions:** wide margins (10–12%), a single column of text, full-page illustrations or photographs facing text.

## Colour
- Warm, earthy and ink-like: ochre, terracotta, sage, deep red and black on cream or uncoated stock.
- Spot colours and duotones. Metallic or foil accents on promotional pieces.

## Imagery
- **Illustration-led**: painterly and woodcut-style marks, Western and Texan iconography, animal and figurative symbols.
- Conceptual photography of found objects.
- Papers showcased through texture, die-cuts and emboss.

## Signature moves
- Visual pun marks (two ideas in one symbol).
- Hand-drawn emblematic logos for restaurants and hospitality (Chili's and Macaroni Grill for Brinker).
- Paper-company promotions that showcase print craft (Weyerhaeuser, James River, International Paper).
- Cream uncoated stock with spot colour and metallic ink.
- Small-caps letterspaced serif credits.
- A single centred illustrative image on a poster.

## 3 notable projects
- **Brinker International: Chili's and Macaroni Grill branding:** illustrative casual-dining identities.
- **Weyerhaeuser / James River / International Paper:** paper promotions in a print-craft showcase format.
- **Mary Kay Cosmetics and Haggar Apparel:** identity and branding programmes for Dallas-based Fortune 500 companies.
