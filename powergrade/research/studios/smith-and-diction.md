# Smith & Diction (Philadelphia)

URL: https://smith-diction.com. Note that smithanddiction.com redirects there over HTTP and refuses HTTPS in this sandbox. The studio was founded in 2015 by Mike Smith (creative director) and Chara Smith (copy chief). Its case studies are published on Medium (medium.com/smith-diction).

## Philosophy
"Thoughtful identities for imaginative clients.®" "Make the one that makes your heart sing." Design and copy are equal partners, hence "Diction". The studio is joyful, humane and slightly irreverent. It writes its own debriefs in a conversational, jokey voice ("Yeehaw brother. Let's get to it."), follows a 10-step process document, and runs one-week brand sprints.

## Typography
- The site is set in **Ringside** (Hoefler&Co): extra-light 200 for body at 20/31 and 36/43, with tight −2.6px tracking for display. Headlines are in a heavy, compressed Ringside: "Thoughtful identities for imaginative clients.®".
- The studio's own wordmark is a swash-heavy, 1970s-style serif with ligatures and loops.
- Client work features unusual, era-evoking pairings by Dayan D'Aniello:
  - western badge type for Cowboy Space;
  - FK Display for Perplexity;
  - letter-spaced Didone caps for Elixr;
  - a woodtype slab for The Westbound.

## Layout & grid
- The site is all black (#000) with white type.
- Work feed: an asymmetric 2-column scatter. Each project is one image of about 33–50% width, offset left or right, with a tiny name, a grey "View Case Study" link and a 20px light description about 30% wide beside or below it.
- Logos page: a giant "Logos & Icons" title in 120px bold at top left, with a micro uppercase note at right. Below it, a **5-column symbol grid**: white marks centred in square cells, each captioned with name, sector and year in 8px.
- Homepage: the centred swash wordmark at about 65% width, then two marquee rows of symbols, then a centred compressed statement and pill buttons in green outline (#2BD94B).

## Signature moves
- **Symbol-led identities**: a dense, clever icon with double meanings (a spur that is also a sunrise; an asterisk that is also a cursor and a book).
- Badge or emblem lockups with text on a circular path.
- Sticker-sheet collateral layouts.
- A logo archive grid (white on black, with year captions).
- Vintage display type with modern restraint.
- Hand-drawn illustration per product variant (Elixr labels).
- Witty copy as part of the identity.

## 3 notable projects
- **Elixr Coffee (2017–18; SCA Design Lab packaging award):** a gift-like hand-held box, an X-crossed Didone wordmark and an illustrated label per blend.
- **Perplexity (2023):** an asterisk symbol (revolving door, open book, cursor) with an FK Display wordmark.
- **Cowboy Space Corp. (2026):** a spur-and-sunrise symbol and a western-badge system for orbital solar satellites.
