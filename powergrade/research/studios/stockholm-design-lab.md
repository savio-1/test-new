# Stockholm Design Lab (Stockholm)

URL: https://www.stockholmdesignlab.se. Founded in 1998 and led by Björn Kusoffsky. Clients include IKEA, SAS, Moderna Museet, Askul, Polestar, the Nobel Prize, Sigma and Oracle Red Bull Racing.

## Philosophy
"Stockholm Design Lab transforms brands and businesses with simple, remarkable ideas." The Sigma case puts it this way: branding is "not about surface but about depth". SDL works holistically and over the long term: strategy, a custom typeface, product branding, architecture, uniforms. Its reduction is Scandinavian, and each identity derives from one source, such as the Nobel medal or the Polestar star.

## Typography
- The site uses **LabGrotesque** (Letters from Sweden) at 16px/1.25 for body and 42px at −0.02em for statements. The "SDL" logo is a small serif.
- Custom families are standard practice:
  - **Alfred Sans and Alfred Serif** for the Nobel Prize, with high-waisted, geometric capitals drawn from the 1902 medal engraving;
  - **Unica77 Polestar Var**, a single weight with variable letter spacing;
  - a bespoke Haglöfs face and numerals, made with Letters from Sweden.
- Type is mostly sentence case at a moderate size. For product marking it is set tiny, factual and widely spaced: "1 size, 1–2 lines under the dash".

## Layout & grid
- Case template:
  - a full-bleed hero image of about 100% × 56%;
  - the project name at left (column 1, about 2–25%);
  - the tagline at about 50–95%, 22px;
  - two narrow body columns of about 21% each at 9px.
- Images run full-width with a 4% margin, or as 2-up 50/50 tiles with a small gap. Generous white bands separate them.
- Hairline-ruled specification tables (symbol / wordmark / typography / colour / marking system), each with a tiny label at left.
- Black image bands are used for product shots.

## Colour
Restrained: white, black, warm greys, plus one heritage accent. Examples: Nobel's gold with a deep oxblood (#6B2E2C) and blush (#EADCD8); Polestar's greys with a single safety yellow (#F5C400); Sigma's black, white and glacier blue.

## Signature moves
- Everything springs from one heritage source (a medal, a star, a 1914 symbol).
- A proprietary typeface as the core asset.
- Typographic marking systems on product: small caps text plus hairlines.
- Specimen cards of giant "Aa / a / Abc" on blush or grey.
- Place-name lockups in stacked caps over landscape photos.
- Hairline-ruled spec tables with micro labels.
- One accent colour against monochrome.

## 3 notable projects
- **The Nobel Prize (2018):** a one-brand system drawn from the 1902 medal. Alfred Sans and Serif, gold plus heritage colours, banners and ceremony print.
- **Polestar (2017–):** the star symbol, Unica77 Polestar Var and a typographic marking system across cars, retail and digital.
- **Sigma (2025; D&AD Yellow Pencil 2026):** strategy, custom typography, packaging, uniforms, architecture and campaigns for the Japanese lens maker.
