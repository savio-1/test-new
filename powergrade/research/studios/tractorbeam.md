# Tractorbeam (Dallas)

URL: https://tractorbeam.com. A full-service agency (design, branding, digital marketing) founded in 1999. I reviewed the homepage and the Stetson, Fordson Hotel, Conserva Culture and Mohonk Mountain House case studies.

## Philosophy
"Tractorbeam creates and grows compelling brands that connect, inspire, and drive real impact." "Everything we do is rooted in our passion for brands, experiences, and possibilities."

The practice has four parts: Strategy, Design, Digital Products and Digital Marketing. Its sweet spot is **hospitality, heritage and lifestyle**: hotels, resorts and legacy American brands, revived through archives, illustration and craft. The identity work is strongly **story- and place-based**: "Honoring the legacy of an industrial icon".

## Typography
- **House style:** **Old Standard TT**, a Didone-ish Victorian serif, for display:
  - 100px with -4px tracking on the home H1;
  - 82px with -3.28px tracking on case titles;
  - sentence case and centred on the home page.
- **Suisse Int'l** is used for UI. The service tags are 14px uppercase chips.
- Client work mixes:
  - **condensed industrial sans** (Fordson's tall "FORDSON / HOTEL");
  - widely tracked small caps (paragraphs set in spaced caps);
  - hand-engraved crests;
  - vertical stacked letters ("F O R D S O N" set vertically).

## Layout & grid
- A black page. Every media block is a **rounded rectangle** (radius about 0.6% of width, ~8px) with 16px gaps and a 3.7% margin.
- Case header in 2 columns: client name small, then a 2-line serif headline in the left 50%, with tag chips below. The descriptive paragraph sits at 60–95%.
- Then a single full-width card (about 3:2), followed by alternating 2-up cards (each about 49%) and full cards.
- The homepage is a 4-column work grid of 4:3 thumbnails with captions (title, arrow and a serif tagline).

## Colour
- The house is black with white text.
- Client palettes are **dusty and heritage-minded**:
  - Fordson's blush (#E8D6D8) and slate blue-grey (#9AA6AE) with oxblood line art;
  - Stetson's oxblood (#3E2A2C) with silver;
  - Whataburger × Magellan's tangerine.

## Signature moves
- Rounded cards on black.
- A Victorian serif headline at -4% tracking.
- A giant condensed wordmark cropped by the bottom edge of a pastel panel.
- An engraved line-art building or crest as the hero asset.
- A paragraph in widely tracked small caps.
- Vertical stacked letters on dark key cards.
- Archive to sketch to final storytelling.

## 3 notable projects
- **Fordson Hotel (Oklahoma City):** a 1916 Ford assembly plant turned hotel. Engraved line illustration, condensed caps, blush and slate.
- **Stetson:** a heritage crest redrawn by hand from the archive, used across retail and campaigns.
- **Conserva Culture:** a culinary goods brand inspired by European tinned-seafood packaging.
