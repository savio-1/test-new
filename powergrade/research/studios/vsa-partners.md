# VSA Partners (Chicago)

URL: https://www.vsapartners.com. An independent branding and activation agency, founded in 1982 and now part of the Meet The People network. I reviewed the homepage and the BNY, Google Cloud, YWCA Chicago and Cole Haan case studies.

## Philosophy
"Creative is currency. We help brands turn it into growth." VSA presents itself as a business partner first: brand systems, campaigns and media that "break through, scale with consistency, and deliver on business outcomes." Its credentials are **systems built for enterprise scale**: Google Cloud's "Cloudstyle", BNY's 240th-anniversary identity and IBM's historic design language. The work is confident, corporate and sharp, not decorative.

## Typography
- The site uses **Inter Tight 600** for display and **Inter 400/500** for text.
- The home H1 is 125px on a 1440px canvas (8.7% of width), tracked at -0.25px, in sentence case with a full stop: "Creative is currency."
- Case titles use a two-tone stack at 64px with 1.0 line-height: the client name in black, the descriptor in 50% grey directly beneath.
- Body text is 19px grey, set ragged right in a column about 45% wide.
- Client work often switches to **condensed heavy all-caps grotesk** for campaigns (BNY's "WE'LL BE READY" triptych, YWCA's "UNTIL JUSTICE JUST IS").

## Layout & grid
- 12 columns with a 50px (3.5%) margin. The nav is right-aligned with the wordmark at top-left.
- Case template:
  - a full-bleed hero photo (16:9);
  - a title block in the left half, with a service list prefixed by "+" in the right third (from about 73%);
  - a 45%-wide intro paragraph;
  - inset media at 93% width with the margins kept;
  - a 2-up split (with a 1px vertical hairline) for before/after logos;
  - 4% white gaps between blocks.

## Colour
- House style is black (#000) and white with one accent: a mint-green arrow (#3DDC97-ish) after "Work →" and case links.
- Client systems carry the colour (BNY navy and teal, Google primaries).

## Imagery
- Hero photography of **architecture-scale in-situ signage**: a dimensional logo shot against the sky with lens flare.
- Documentary event photography.
- Triptych digital OOH in a subway.
- A wide "system wall" collage of dozens of applications on grey.
- Black-and-white reportage for cause work.

## Signature moves
- A two-tone title stack (black client name, grey claim).
- A "+" service list in the right third.
- A before/after logo split with a centre hairline.
- A triptych OOH where one word sits on each panel.
- A system-wall mosaic proving scale.
- Condensed all-caps campaign headlines over darkened photography.
- A single mint arrow as the only house accent.

## 3 notable projects
- **BNY (2024):** "BNY Mellon" simplified to BNY, keeping the arrow. A heavy grotesk wordmark, navy and teal, and a "We'll be ready" campaign.
- **Google Cloud (2019 onward):** the "Super Cloud" anchor and the Cloudstyle framework across web, events and print.
- **YWCA Chicago (2020):** the "Until Justice Just Is" platform. Orange and black condensed type, plus black-and-white documentary photography.
