# Wolff Olins (London / New York)

URL: https://www.wolffolins.com. A global brand consultancy founded in 1965 and now part of Omnicom. I reviewed the homepage and the Blank Street, Lloyds, Decathlon and New York Botanical Garden case studies.

## Philosophy
"We create transformative brands that move businesses, people and the world forward." WO is ideas-led and works from culture outwards. Its well-known disruptions include the London 2012 identity, (RED) and Tate. The case studies lead with **business ambition and behaviour** ("Brand Lloyds as an operating system"), and every case has a metrics strip. Brands are treated as **living systems** with verbal identity, motion and people, not as fixed logos.

## Typography
- **House style:**
  - **Monument Grotesk** for the UI and headings: 55px case titles and 30px subheads, regular weight, sentence case;
  - **Untitled Serif** for body and statements ("We create transformative brands…" at about 40px, with key words underlined);
  - a custom **WO** display face for the scribbly lowercase "wolff olins" mark.
- Client work is **expressive and type-led**:
  - Lloyds' "Cancara" serif with swash italics;
  - NYBG's heavy display grotesk with all-caps posters;
  - Decathlon's tight caps.

## Layout & grid
- The nav is a floating rounded **pill** (about 55% width, centred, translucent dark) at the top.
- The case page is **black**. The intro is 2 columns: the client name at 55px top-left, with Industry and What We Did tags beneath; the serif body text sits at 50–95% width.
- Below the intro is a **stats row** of 3 large numbers.
- Media rhythm:
  - full-width (96%) images and films;
  - **an asymmetric single image in the right half only**, leaving the left half black;
  - 2-up splits with about 0.5% gutters.

## Colour
- The house is black and white with a **signal yellow (#FFF200-ish)** accent for the cookie bar, play buttons and tags.
- Client palettes are bold and unexpected: Lloyds' mint, NYBG's orange, magenta and violet, Blank Street's sage.

## Imagery
- Fashion-editorial street photography with real people and flash:
  - a person holding a printed sheet up to camera;
  - a kid on a scooter in front of a green panel.
- Architectural storefronts.
- Motion films.
- Product macro shots (drinks, cups).

## Case-study presentation
1. Hero in-situ.
2. Title, tags and stats.
3. Brand film.
4. Idea or strategy line.
5. The identity in use as **posters held or worn by people**.
6. A split of environment and macro detail.
7. Storefront and interior.
8. Typography and verbal-identity samples.
9. Social and app.
10. Results.

## Signature moves
- Black canvas with a half-width image offset right.
- A serif statement with underlined keywords.
- A stats triptych of big numerals.
- A rounded pill nav and tag chips.
- People holding posters to camera (brand as a human prop).
- A bold display headline on a flat-colour panel cropped into a photo (the "Portfolio expert" block).
- Tag chips with thin outlines.

## 3 notable projects
- **Decathlon (2024):** "Rewriting sport's playbook". A new symbol and the "wonders of sport" platform, with retail environments.
- **Lloyds (2025):** a brand operating system with the "Cancara" serif, mint and green, and the line "Everyone has a next step forward".
- **Blank Street (2025):** a sage-green coffee identity with a square-dot wordmark and dimensional blade signs.
