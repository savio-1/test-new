Conversion runs from the local app. The models need a GPU, which runs on Hugging Face and is called from your computer. This page can open and render models you already have.

    The .glb opens in Blender, Unity, Unreal, three.js and most 3D viewers. For path-traced studio shots outside this page, run render.py on the model.

    Reconstruction by TRELLIS.2, Hunyuan3D 2.1 or Stable Fast 3D, run on Hugging Face GPUs. Check each model's license before commercial use. The sides and back that the photo can't see are generated by the model, so they are plausible rather than exact.
